# NOTE-003 Review Output Must Be Critical and Actionable

- Status: approved
- Type: review-preference
- Durability: high

## Preference

Reviews should be objective and direct, explicitly call out flaws, and provide concrete improvement actions. Findings should be prioritized by impact and tied to clear fix directions rather than generic advice.

## Apply When

- Running quality audits, design critiques, or `vet`-style review.
- Producing remediation guidance after assessment.
